Clash Snake Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Clash Snake (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published on Google Play by Azax, an individual developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at azax.developer@gmail.com.

Summary

Clash Snake does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party data collection. It works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress and preferences, the App stores game data locally on your device only, such as:

  • Best score and the total score used for your player level
  • Coin balance
  • Unlocked and selected skins
  • Achievements and the title you select
  • Game statistics, such as games played, kills, longest length, food eaten, time played and Battle Royale results
  • The player name you type, if any. It is saved on your device to fill in the name field next time and is shown only on the in-game leaderboard against computer-controlled snakes
  • A random player tag created on your device for display in the game
  • Settings, such as music, sound volume and control layout

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive information, including names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or usage data.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Third-party services

The current version of the App does not include any third-party services that collect data, such as advertising or analytics SDKs. If a future version adds such services, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe what data is collected and by whom.

Children's privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Data retention

We do not store any user data on servers. Game data stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Deleting your data

All game data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at azax.developer@gmail.com.
